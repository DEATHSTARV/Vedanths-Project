# Candidate identities

Made by Vedanth in Computationally Yours: Out of One's Place, Srishti Manipal Institute, Interim 2026.

**The press:** https://DEATHSTARV.github.io/Vedanths-Project/ — set the dials, choose a seed, download.

## Radio

*I am a transmission from the future, endlessly warning the present about things that have already happened.*

### The work

This system looks at memory as something that changes every time it is repeated. It creates layered forms that slowly break apart, overlap and change as fragments of earlier states are carried forward. The final artefact becomes a kind of visual record of a message that has been transmitted so many times that it is no longer possible to recover the original.

## Pixel

*I am a fragment of information escaping an internet that has become too full to contain itself.*

### The work

This system explores what happens when information starts escaping the structures that were built to contain it. Pixels begin as small, ordered parts of a grid, but gradually move, multiply, cluster and spill outside their original boundaries. The final artefact becomes a kind of digital ecosystem that has grown beyond the screen it came from.

## Artifact

*I am a machine from 4026 CE trying to reconstruct a vanished civilisation from the objects it left behind.*

### The work

This system starts with fragments and tries to build meaning from them. Objects are repeatedly interpreted, changed and connected until they begin forming structures that resemble a civilisation. The further the system goes, the more its reconstruction moves away from the original evidence, showing how easily meaning can change when context is lost.
